About Sourabh Bhatt

Sourabh Bhatt is an Indian Artist with 25+ years of artistic experience, 18+ years of Fine Arts teaching, and 8+ years of teaching IBDP Visual Arts, IGCSE Art & Design, and Cambridge Checkpoint (IGCSE-CP). He is a Next-Gen Digital Art Mentor, Art Career Counsellor, and the Founder & Director of Aalok Art Gallery.

For over 18 years, he has helped students, artists, and beginners transform their creativity into confidence by blending traditional Fine Arts with modern AI-powered digital tools. His work extends beyond the classroom through portfolio mentoring, exhibition curation, and international creative guidance, helping learners build globally competitive portfolios and prepare for universities, design schools, and future-ready artistic careers.

With participation in 100+ national and international art exhibitions, including showcases across London, Brazil, Denmark, and Thailand, he continues to connect traditional artistic excellence with the future of digital creativity through education, innovation, and creative leadership.
